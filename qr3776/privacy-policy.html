QR3776

How QR3776 handles user information.

Effective: December 16, 2025Updated: October 4, 2026

1. Scanning

QR3776 uses the camera or a user-selected image to scan QR codes and barcodes. Recognition uses Google ML Kit Barcode Scanning and runs on-device. Lab 3776 does not send camera images, selected images, or decoded scan contents to a Lab 3776 server.

Google's ML Kit disclosure states that the SDK may collect device/app information, per-installation identifiers, performance and API metrics, feature/event information, and error codes for diagnostics and usage analytics. Google states that this data is encrypted in transit and is not shared by ML Kit with third parties.

ML Kit data disclosure

2. Local history

Normal scan results are stored in an on-device database for the History feature and can be deleted in the app. The database is excluded from Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer.

TOTP/HOTP authentication QR codes are not saved by default. If saving is enabled, authentication data is encrypted with AES-GCM using an Android Keystore-protected key. Unsupported authentication/migration QR codes are transient.

Wi-Fi QR codes may contain passwords, so supported versions do not save them to normal history. They are kept only in memory for immediate post-scan actions. Wi-Fi QR history saved by older versions is removed on upgrade.

3. Settings and external actions

Theme, language, behavior settings, and a local Supporter Badge ownership cache are stored on-device. These settings do not contain authentication secrets or Wi-Fi passwords. Preference data may participate in Android backup or device transfer.

When the user explicitly chooses an action, a scanned value may be passed to a browser, share target, authenticator app, or Android Wi-Fi functionality. The receiving app or service then controls further handling.

4. Google Play Billing

QR3776 offers an optional one-time Supporter Badge through Google Play Billing. Google Play handles payment processing. QR3776 receives product and purchase-state information but does not access or store credit-card or bank-account details.

5. Advertising

The current QR3776 source does not include an advertising SDK. Some earlier versions used Google AdMob and may still be subject to AdMob data processing.
